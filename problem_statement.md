### 1. Vision & Core Objective
The **Digital Twin Challenge 2026**, organized by **Happiest Health**, takes place at the *Reimagining and Reforming Healthcare in India Summit - 2026* in Bengaluru. A Digital Twin in healthcare is defined as a dynamic, virtual replica of a patient continuously updated with real-time wearable data and Electronic Health Records (EHR) to simulate physiology, forecast adverse events, and personalize treatment.

Participants are challenged to build a **proof-of-concept (PoC) Digital Twin model** predicting a specific, localized health outcome.

---

### 2. Technical Scope & Requirements
* **Condition Focus:** Teams must select one specific chronic or lifestyle condition prevalent in India (such as **Type 2 Diabetes**, **Hypertension**, or **Cardiovascular risk**).
* **Dual Data Stream Ingestion:**
  1. **Static / Historical EHR Data:** Simulated medical records covering demographics, past diagnoses, lab results, and genetic markers.
  2. **Dynamic / Real-time Sensor Data:** Time-series biometric data from consumer wearables and IoT sensors (e.g., heart rate variability, continuous glucose monitors, sleep stages, and step counts).
* **Algorithmic Output:** An algorithmic model that processes both streams to predict an adverse event (e.g., forecasting a blood glucose spike 2 hours in advance or identifying a high-risk cardiovascular anomaly from a week of sleep and heart-rate data).
* **Clinician Interface:** A conceptual UI dashboard demonstrating how a physician interacts with the patient twin.

---

### 3. Data Compliance & "Sandbox Rules"
* **Privacy Regulations:** Due to regulations like the **DPDP Act** and **HIPAA**, real patient data access is restricted.
* **Mandatory Data Sources:** Models must be trained and tested using anonymized, open-source, or synthetic datasets.
  * **EHR Sources:** Synthetic patient tools (e.g., **Synthea**) or anonymized databases (e.g., **MIMIC-IV**).
  * **Wearable Sources:** Open-source datasets or synthetic time-series mimicking **Apple Health**, **Google Fit**, or medical IoT devices.

---

### 4. Submission Format & Public GitHub Requirements
* **Repository Folder Naming:** `Team Name_College Name`.
* **Access Level:** The repository link and all attached files/links must be set to **Public** without permission barriers.
* **Mandatory README.md Contents:**
  * Team details and college/incubator information.
  * Project Title, Problem Statement, and Healthcare Use Case.
  * Technical Stack and AI/ML model/framework details.
  * **Demo Video:** A 15–20 minute (or minimum 20-minute) demonstration video, recorded as a presentation or uploaded as an unlisted YouTube link in the README.
  * Open-source license details.
  * System Architecture Diagram (PDF/PPT format).
  * Project Presentation Deck covering methodology and outcomes (PDF/PPT format).
* **Form Submission Details:** Team leader's name, phone number, email ID, and public GitHub URL.

### 5. Challenge Stages & Timeline
* **Phase 1: Prototype & Code Submission:**
  * **Submission Window:** 18 September 2026 to 20 October 2026.

* **Phase 2: Virtual Jury Presentation:**
  * Top 10 shortlisted teams virtually demonstrate their working prototype and technical implementation to an expert jury.
* **Phase 3: Summit Showcase (Grand Finale):**
  * Top 5 finalist teams present live at the summit in Bengaluru on **20 November 2026**.
  * **Travel & Accommodation:** Happiest Health sponsors travel and stay for 2 members per finalist team.
